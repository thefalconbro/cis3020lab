Welcome to My Persona Fan Page

This fan page is dedicated to the Persona video game series developed by Atlus. I enjoy the series because it combines turn-based role-playing gameplay with meaningful stories about identity, personal growth, and facing inner struggles. The mix of everyday life simulation and supernatural themes makes Persona stand out from other RPGs.
