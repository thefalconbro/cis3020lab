How did you become a fan?

I became a fan after playing Persona 5 Royal because I had heard it was a great story-driven RPG. I ended up loving the way the game ties in myths and historical figures with great storytelling, along with the amazing characters and their development. After finishing it, I went on to play the other Persona games and really enjoyed their stories and characters as well.

What do you love most about it?

I love the engaging characters, the amazing music, and the way the games incorporate myth and historical figures into each character’s Persona. I also really enjoy the balance between dungeon crawling and the social simulation aspect, since it makes the gameplay feel varied and keeps the story connected to character growth.

What would you recommend to someone just getting started?

I would recommend that anyone just getting started play Persona 5 Royal first because it feels the most up-to-date and mechanically the smoothest. After that, I would suggest Persona 3 Reload, followed by Persona 4 Golden. Once they are comfortable with those, they could explore some of the older entries in the series.

Do you have any favorite fan communities or creators?

I enjoy looking at fan art and cosplays that people like to post. I also like listening to songs inspired by the games, including remixes and covers based on the original soundtrack. The fan community is very creative and adds another layer of enjoyment to the series.
